About & responsible AI

The project

A reusable NLP asset for AI University: Building Reusable NLP Assets. It reads a student's free-text message and predicts one of six concern categories and one of four urgency levels, each with a confidence score, plus a recommended action looked up from the category.

Group 6 stretch goal: a dashboard of emerging categories of student concerns. Named integration: Group 5 (student feedback analysis) feeds Group 6.

Responsible-AI commitments

  • Human in the loop. Output is advisory; staff make every decision.
  • Confidence shown. Anything below 0.5 is marked for manual reading.
  • No real student data. Training data is synthetic and template-based; Group 5's real reviews are public (EduRABSA, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
  • Local processing. Messages are analysed on this machine and not stored.
  • Honest limits. Synthetic data makes category scores optimistic; mixed praise-and-complaint text and adjacent urgency levels are the main failure modes.
